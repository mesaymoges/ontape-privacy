Ontape privacy policy

Ontape: Screen Recorder for Android. Last updated 11 November 2026.

The short version

Ontape collects no personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics, and no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Screen capture: only after you start a recording and accept Android's screen capture prompt. It is used to make the video.
  • Microphone and device sound: only while recording, and only if you turn these on. They are used for the video's soundtrack.
  • Notifications: to show the recording controls (Pause, Stop) while a recording runs.
  • Storage: recordings are saved as normal video files in Movies/Ontape on your phone.

Where your recordings go

Recordings stay on your phone. Ontape never uploads, shares or sells them. A recording leaves your phone only if you choose to share it yourself, for example with the Share button, and then the app you share it with handles it under its own policy.

Data sharing and retention

No data is collected, so none is shared with third parties or kept by us. You can delete recordings in the app or in your gallery at any time. Uninstalling Ontape removes its settings; your saved videos stay in Movies/Ontape until you delete them.

Children

Ontape is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
